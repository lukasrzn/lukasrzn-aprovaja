====================================================

- When a user clicks “Fazer simulado”
- The system enters a loading state
- BUT the page gets stuck in infinite loading
- No questions are displayed
- The simulation never starts

YOUR MISSION
====================================================

You must:

1. Identify why the simulated exam is stuck in infinite loading
2. Fix the question loading/rendering pipeline
3. Ensure questions are properly fetched and displayed

====================================================
EXPECTED BEHAVIOR (CORRECT FLOW)
====================================================

When user clicks “Fazer simulado”:

1. System loads simulation data
2. Questions are fetched from backend or generated by AI

CURRENT BROKEN BEHAVIOR

ROOT CAUSE INVESTIGATION

- AI generation failing or timing out

- Request not completing (pending promise)
